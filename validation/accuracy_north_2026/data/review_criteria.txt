Manual-look criteria, northern assessment (written 27 Nov 2026, before any northern curve was fetched).
Applied to every point the rules call "unclear", blind to the map. Months Sep..Aug, values x1000 (offset-corrected NDVI).
Same as the southern criteria except the winter season runs to May and must be over by July (the north run's season is
1 Nov 2025 - 30 Jun 2026), plus the northern notes at the end.
- winter_crop: one clear seasonal rise and fall with its peak in Dec-May: peak >= 350, rising at least 150 above the
  autumn level before it and falling back at least half way after it by July (a single-month spike does not count).
  A crop that emerges in Feb-Apr, peaks in Apr-May and is dry by June-July counts (late or spring-sown crops inside the
  map's season, e.g. late barley or spring chickpea).
- summer_only: the clear rise and fall peaks in Jun-Oct (>= 350), or is still green (>= 350) in July, and Dec-Apr
  stays low (< 300).
- perennial: >= 300 in most months (9+) without a winter rise-and-fall that meets the winter_crop test.
- no_crop: max < 330, or only an isolated single-month spike.
- water: mostly negative.
- unclear: anything else (two competing seasons of similar size, gaps that hide the peak, weak 300-350 winter peak).
Northern notes:
- Missing Jan-Feb months (cloud or snow in the mountains) do not by themselves make a point unclear if the rise before
  and the fall after the peak are both visible.
- Several dips and regrowths inside Dec-May (grazing or fodder cuts) still count as winter_crop if the season as a whole
  meets the test.
- A winter crop followed by a summer crop counts as winter_crop.
Every manual label is recorded with a one-line reason. The rule label is kept beside it.
