Land-use check, northern assessment: amendment 1 (written 27 Nov 2026 ~18:40 UTC, AFTER the map key was opened and
BEFORE any imagery was looked at).

Why: with the key open, 132 of 234 "not cropland" points had a winter-crop NDVI curve, 110 of them in the Kurdistan
Region, mostly peaking in May. In the north, natural grassland and rangeland green up in spring and dry by June like a
rain-fed crop, so the NDVI rules alone cannot separate them. The reference "winter crop" therefore needs a land-use test.

Amended reference: a point is "winter crop" only if (a) its curve label is winter_crop (labels_final.csv, unchanged)
and (b) the land at the point is farmed. A winter_crop curve on land judged not farmed becomes "no crop" (natural
vegetation). Unclear curves stay unclear whatever the land use. Both the original and the amended figures are reported.

Which points: every point whose final curve label is winter_crop or unclear (380), shuffled with seed 20261130,
shown with no map class, map colours or ids that reveal the class.

Imagery: Esri World Imagery (undated in the viewer; shows land use, not this season's crop), zoom 16 view about
500 m across with the point marked, plus a zoom 18 close-up.

Classes, judged at the marked point:
- farmed: the point lies inside a cultivated parcel: straight or regular field edges, plough or drill lines, a
  patchwork of fields with different tones, terraces, or an irrigation pattern; includes fallow-looking parcels inside
  a field pattern.
- not_farmed: the point lies on land with no field pattern: natural slopes, rangeland, scrub or forest, rock, gullies,
  river beds, built-up land, roads, water.
- unsure: the point sits on a field edge or boundary, the imagery is blurred or cloudy, or the pattern is ambiguous
  (for example faint old field lines on rangeland).
Each look gets a one-line note. Unsure points are reported both ways (as farmed / as not farmed), as for unclear curves.

Amendment 2 (written 27 Nov 2026 ~19:00 UTC, after the imagery look of amendment 1 and BEFORE any field outline was
read): resolving "unsure" land use with Fields of the World (FTW).

Why: 94 of 380 imagery looks were "unsure", mostly flat plains with no visible field edges, which leaves the bounds too
wide (winter-crop area 1.78-3.45 Mha).

Source: FTW's Iraq file (source.coop results-by-admin-conf, admin:country_code=IQ/Iraq.pmtiles), as served to the app
from tiles.iraqfarmshealth.com/fields/Iraq.pmtiles, layer "2025", zoom 13 (its most detailed level). FTW predicts field
boundaries from Sentinel-2 with its own model, independent of WorldCereal.

Test: a point is inside a field if it lies inside an FTW 2025 polygon (point-in-polygon on the zoom-13 tile geometry).
Rule: for points whose imagery land use is "unsure", inside a field -> farmed, outside -> not_farmed. Imagery
judgements of "farmed" and "not_farmed" are NOT changed. Layer "2024" is read too and reported as a sensitivity check.
The FTW answer is also recorded for all 380 points, and its agreement with the imagery labels is reported.
